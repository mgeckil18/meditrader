# Value of information (Grossman-Stiglitz)

**Literature.** If information were free and everyone had it, prices would reflect it fully and nobody would be paid for gathering it, so nobody would gather it (Grossman & Stiglitz 1980). Information has value only because it is costly and unevenly spread (Stigler 1961).

**Mechanism in the model.** Information travels physically: a ship brings the prices of the port it left, rumours spread with a daily probability, and beliefs widen with age ([information model](../design/information-model.md)).

**What appeared.**
- With instant information, the player bot earned about a bond's return (2.5-3.6% a year). [Experiment 01](../experiments/01-instant-information.md).
- With realistic delay, an instantly informed bot earned 11-23 points a year more than a bot with the same information as the NPCs. [Experiment 02](../experiments/02-information-delay.md).
- In equilibrium, with free entry, the informed advantage stayed at 5-13 points while ordinary margins were competed away. [Experiment 03](../experiments/03-entry-speed.md).

**Player text.**
> If everyone knew everything, nobody would make a profit. Profit belongs to whoever knows first and knows better. News costs money; not knowing costs more.
