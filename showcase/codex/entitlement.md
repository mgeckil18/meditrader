# Entitlement and famine

**Literature.** Famine is not only a shortage of food but a collapse of some groups' ability to command it (Sen 1981; source flagged for verification in the Turkish codex).

**Mechanism in the model.** Urban demand is split into three income classes with budgets from historical consumption data (Linear Expenditure System). When grain prices rise, each class pays for grain first and spends what is left.

**What appeared.** In a 30% wheat shortfall, the poor stopped buying cloth for about five months (12-month average −42%) and the middle class cut back 10%. The rich bought 7% *more*, because the cloth the poor could no longer afford became cheaper. [Experiment 04](../experiments/04-cloth-artifact.md).

**Player text.**
> A famine is not only a lack of bread; it is a question of who can still pay for it. The rich eat in hard years too, and pick up the goods that have suddenly become cheap.
