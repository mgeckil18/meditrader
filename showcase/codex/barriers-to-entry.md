# Barriers to entry and economic rent

**Literature.** Where entry is free, economic profit is competed down to the cost of capital plus a risk premium. Where entry is hard, insiders earn a rent (Bain 1956; Stigler 1968).

**Mechanism in the model.** Merchants enter when the median return of active merchants exceeds the bond rate plus a risk premium. They exit when their own return stays below the bond rate. Only the *speed* of entry differs between setups.

**What appeared.** Sector return over all capital ever invested: 19-26% with slow entry, 6-11% with moderate entry (the target, bond + risk), 1-2% with herd entry. [Experiment 03](../experiments/03-entry-speed.md).

**Design use.** Guild membership, citizenship and trade privileges are barriers to entry. Their value in the game is the rent they protect, which the model can measure.

**Player text.**
> If a trade is hard to enter (guilds, privileges, large capital), those inside earn extra. If it is easy, the profit is shared among everyone until little more than a bond's return is left.
