# Pork cycle

**Literature.** When capacity is decided on past profits and takes time to arrive, the industry overshoots in both directions: profit attracts entry, capacity piles up, profit collapses, firms leave, profit returns (Hanau 1928; Coase & Fowler 1935).

**Mechanism in the model.** Entry and exit decisions look at the last 12 months of returns. Nothing schedules a cycle.

**What appeared.** The number of merchants cycles in every entry structure. Under herd entry it swings between 5 and 120 merchants. [Experiment 03](../experiments/03-entry-speed.md). The cycle is kept as a phenomenon; herd entry was rejected for other reasons: its micro-behaviour is unrealistic, and its swings hit an artificial cap.

**Player text.**
> When a trade looks profitable, everyone enters; there are too many ships, profit collapses, everyone leaves; then it is profitable again. Entering while everyone is leaving is the bold merchant's game.
