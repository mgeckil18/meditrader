# Survivorship bias

**Literature.** Looking only at those who survived overstates the success of a strategy (classic example: Wald's 1943 analysis of returning bombers; source not yet verified). It is widely documented in finance.

**Mechanism in the model.** NPCs judge a trade by the merchants they can see in port, as real people would. Our measurement uses the ledger: all capital that ever entered, including bankrupts and those who left.

**What appeared.** Under herd entry, survivors' median return looked like 12-22% a year while the sector as a whole earned 1-2%. [Experiment 03](../experiments/03-entry-speed.md).

**Player text.**
> The rich merchants you see in port make trade look more profitable than it is, because you cannot see the ones who went under.
